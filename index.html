Research Interests

Moving information with light, classical and quantum.

Quantum light sources

Photon pairs · squeezed light
The 3D view needs WebGL, which this browser has turned off.

In a high-Q Si₃N₄ microring, a χ⁽³⁾ material, two pump photons can annihilate and create a signal–idler pair on neighbouring resonances (spontaneous four-wave mixing). In a periodically poled lithium niobate waveguide, a χ⁽²⁾ material, one pump photon at twice the frequency splits into a pair (spontaneous parametric down-conversion), and the poling period Λ sets the phase matching. In both, detecting one photon heralds the other.

Driven harder, the same nonlinear processes produce squeezed light: one quadrature's noise falls below the vacuum level. How far below depends on loss, so source purity and low-loss design decide what is usable.

Quantum frequency conversion

Quantum interconnect

Most quantum memories emit photons at visible or near-infrared wavelengths, where optical fiber is lossy. Quantum frequency conversion shifts a single photon to a telecom wavelength while keeping its quantum state, so it can travel kilometers of fiber.

A strong pump and a χ⁽²⁾ waveguide carry out the shift through difference-frequency generation. The practical targets are high conversion efficiency and low added noise from the pump, both set by the waveguide design.

Link · co-packaged optics, 4 channels

One continuous-wave laser pumps a Si₃N₄ microresonator that turns it into a comb of evenly spaced wavelengths. On the silicon transmitter, a tree of ring-assisted and asymmetric MZI filters sorts the comb lines into lanes, a ring modulator tuned to each line writes its data, and a mirrored tree folds every channel back onto one output fiber.

The scene below follows four of those channels between two co-packaged modules. Each ring modulator writes its color's bits, and at the receiver a matching ring filter drops that color into its own photodetector. Packaging these chiplets next to the driver and receiver electronics keeps the electrical path short, which is what sets energy per bit in chip-to-chip and memory links.

Seoul National University (SNU), Q-SPIN Lab, Republic of Korea

QFC chip · ⁸⁷Rb 780 nm → telecom 1300 nm
The 3D view needs WebGL, which this browser has turned off.
Principle · laterally split-poled domains
  • Designed an on-chip quantum frequency conversion (QFC) device that shifts 780 nm single photons from ⁸⁷Rb atoms to the 1300 nm telecom band by difference-frequency generation with a 1950 nm pump, so the photons can travel through low-loss optical fiber. The photon and the pump are combined in a fiber WDM before the chip and the leftover pump is filtered after it, so the chip itself needs only one straight waveguide.
  • Light is guided by a Si₃N₄ strip under a bonded z-cut thin-film lithium niobate (TFLN) layer. Modal phase matching places the 780 nm light in the TM10 mode and the 1300 nm and 1950 nm light in TM00, so momentum is conserved without periodic poling.
  • Because TM10 is odd across the waveguide, a uniform χ⁽²⁾ makes the nonlinear overlap cancel. Laterally split-poled domains, two LN domains of opposite sign split at the waveguide centre, make the integrand even, so both lobes add and the coupling κ becomes nonzero.
  • The internal conversion efficiency follows , so the design raises through the mode overlap while keeping the propagation loss low.

Linewidth engineering

Refs: Z. Vernon et al., Opt. Lett. 42, 3638 (2017) · Y. Liu et al., Opt. Lett. 45, 73 (2020)

  • Two asymmetric MZI couplers with a path difference of πR address alternate ring resonances, so the pump linewidth (low Q) and the signal/idler linewidths (high Q) are set independently.
  • With Qs,i/Qp ≈ 3 the joint spectrum becomes nearly separable, lifting the purity above the 93% bound of a conventional ring to about 99% in theory; Liu et al. measured 95 ± 1.5% by g(2).

Coupling engineering

Ref: X. Jia et al., Nat. Photon. 20, 428 (2026)

  • Two asymmetric MZIs with twice the ring FSR address alternate resonances: MZI 1 critically couples the pump in, while MZI 2 strongly over-couples the signal and idler out to the drop port; the escape efficiency reaches 0.975 while the pump threshold rises only linearly.
  • An auxiliary ring (FSR ratio 3:4 with the principal ring) hybridizes with the idler resonance, so squeezers across a 6-inch Si3N4 wafer can be spectrally aligned; Jia et al. measured −2.7 dB single-mode and −3.3 dB two-mode squeezing, or −7.5 dB and −9.7 dB inferred on chip.
